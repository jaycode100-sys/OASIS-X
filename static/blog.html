Feature June 21, 2026

Telegram & WhatsApp Integration Now Available

We're excited to announce that OASIS-X now supports real-time notifications via Telegram and WhatsApp. Network administrators can now receive instant alerts when critical faults are detected, along with daily summary reports.

To enable notifications, navigate to your profile settings and add your Telegram username and WhatsApp number. The system will automatically send alerts when the network state transitions to CRITICAL.

Incident June 19, 2026

Fibre Cut Detected on Lagos-Abuja Route

A fibre cut was detected on the Lagos-Abuja trunk route at 14:32 WAT. The system automatically triggered the healing protocol and rerouted traffic through backup paths. Service was restored within 45 minutes.

Root cause: Physical cable damage during road construction. The affected segment has been marked for repair.
