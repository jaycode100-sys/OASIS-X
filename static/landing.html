// How It Works

Detect. Diagnose. Heal.

01

Detect

Continuous OSNR, BER, and latency monitoring across every fibre segment. Anomalies are flagged in milliseconds.

02

Diagnose

LLM-powered root cause analysis classifies fault type, urgency, and recommends the optimal healing protocol.

03

Heal

Autonomous decision engine triggers corrective actions — rerouting traffic, adjusting power levels, or dispatching field crews.

// Capabilities

Built for Nigerian Networks

Real-Time Monitoring

Continuous OSNR, BER, and latency tracking across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Kano with NCC QoS baseline comparison.

AI Fault Diagnosis

LLM-powered root cause analysis with confidence scoring, urgency classification, and actionable repair recommendations.

Autonomous Healing

Decision engine maps network states to corrective actions — automatically escalating from DEGRADING to CRITICAL responses.

Environmental Modeling

Simulates harmattan dust, rain attenuation, generator failures, and peak congestion patterns specific to Nigerian conditions.

NCC Compliance

Automated compliance reporting against Nigerian Communications Commission Quality of Service standards for ISPs.

Digital Twin

Live network topology visualization showing cable routes, node health, and active fault propagation across the infrastructure.
